Our Story

Born from a Love of Thoughtful Giving

Pineapples & Artichokes began the way the best things do — out of a genuine desire to make someone feel truly seen. Ariana Henderson has always believed that a gift is a love language, and that the details matter far more than most people realize.

"I want every person who receives one of my baskets to feel like they were thought about for longer than five minutes."

What started as handmade gifts for friends and family quickly grew into something much bigger. Word spread about the beautifully color-coordinated baskets — the way the crinkle paper matched the ribbon, the way the items all spoke the same visual language, the signature scalloped hang tag that made even the outside feel like an experience.

Today, Pineapples & Artichokes serves clients across the region — for birthdays, housewarmings, Mother's Day, Valentine's Day, school auctions, and every meaningful occasion in between. Each basket is assembled by Ariana personally, with the same care and attention she gave the very first one.
